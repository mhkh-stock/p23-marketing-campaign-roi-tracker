# P23 — Data dictionary + known quirks

## The campaign file — `data/raw/campaigns_SAMPLE.csv`

SAMPLE campaign ROI file for the fictional Pakistani fashion
e-commerce store **StyleHub.pk**, Thu 2026-01-01 → Tue 2026-06-30
(6 months, 181 days). 12 campaigns across 7 channels,
PKR 5,693,000 total spend, PKR 17,131,500 total revenue, overall
ROAS 3.01x, 8,703,740 impressions, 340,334 clicks, 4,139 orders.
Generated by `src/generate_data.py` (seed 20261119) with
deliberately different behaviour per campaign — a clear winner
(Influencer Fest — Ayesha, ROAS 6.76x, and the tiny email
campaigns at 14–18x), two clear losers to kill (Flash Sale SMS
and Brand Awareness — Meta, both ROAS < 1 with payback longer
than their flight), one traffic-heavy but poor-converting channel
(TikTok Ads: the most clicks in the account at a 0.51%
conversion rate) and a proven Search / Shopping core at ROAS
3.12–3.51x — so the report has a real kill/scale story, a real
channel story and a real monthly story to surface.

| Column | Type | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| campaign_id | text | `CMP-01` … `CMP-12`; the campaign key |
| campaign_name | text | The campaign's name |
| channel | text | One of 7: Google Search, Google Shopping, Meta Ads, TikTok Ads, Influencer, Email, SMS/WhatsApp |
| start_date | text | `yyyy-mm-dd`; the first day of the flight |
| end_date | text | `yyyy-mm-dd`; the last day of the flight |
| days | integer | Flight length in days, end − start + 1 |
| spend_pkr | integer | The campaign's total spend in PKR |
| impressions | integer | Times the ad was shown (for Email / SMS: messages sent) |
| clicks | integer | Clicks on the ad |
| conversions | integer | Orders attributed to the campaign |
| revenue_pkr | integer | Revenue in PKR, rounded to the nearest PKR 100 |

## The processed files — `data/processed/`

- `campaigns_enriched.csv` — all 12 campaigns as read by
  `src/analyze.py`, with every metric and the Kill / Scale / Keep
  call.
- `channel_summary.csv` — one row per channel (7 rows): campaigns,
  spend, traffic, revenue, all rates, gross profit, payback.
- `monthly_summary.csv` — one row per month (6 rows): campaigns
  active, spend, revenue, conversions, ROAS (day-share split).
- `funnel_summary.csv` — 3 rows: impressions → clicks →
  conversions, with conversion at each step.

## Known quirks (read before quoting a number)

- **Email's ROAS is real, its scale is not.** Email shows 15.98x
  ROAS on only PKR 83,000 of spend and 301 orders, and Winback —
  Lapsed Buyers shows 18.17x on PKR 38,000. The rates are real;
  the list is too small to move the business, and a small email
  list cannot absorb a big budget. Judge Email on its rate and
  its order count together.
- **The 45% gross margin is an assumption, not a measured
  margin.** StyleHub.pk sells fashion at roughly 45% gross margin
  after product cost. Every payback and gross-profit number uses
  it, and the workbook shows the unadjusted revenue next to every
  adjusted number so the reader can re-run it at their own
  margin.
- **Monthly numbers are a day-share split, not a platform
  report.** A campaign's spend, revenue and conversions are
  spread evenly across its flight days. A campaign that actually
  spent unevenly across its flight will read slightly differently
  month to month — the 6-month totals are exact either way.
- **For Email and SMS/WhatsApp, "impressions" means messages
  sent.** CTR for those channels is clicks / messages sent, not
  clicks / ad views. The funnel is the same shape, measured the
  way those channels are actually bought.
- **January–February look weak for a reason.** Those months
  carried the Brand Awareness flight (ROAS 0.87x) and the TikTok
  launch (0.35% conversion). The March jump to 3.09x is the Eid
  campaigns landing, not a change in the method.
