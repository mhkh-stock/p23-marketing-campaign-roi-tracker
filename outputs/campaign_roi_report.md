## 6. The five recommendations

1. **Kill 2 campaigns now: Brand Awareness — Meta (ROAS 0.87, payback 115.27 days on a 45-day flight — it never paid back), Flash Sale SMS (ROAS 0.79, payback 56.14 days on a 20-day flight — it never paid back). Together they burned PKR 740,000 for PKR 637,400 back. Stop them before the next rupee is spent.**
2. **Scale the winners: Winback — Lapsed Buyers is the best campaign in the account by ROAS (18.17x, payback 4.52 days, PKR 690,600 on PKR 38,000 spend) — but it runs on a small email list, so it cannot absorb a big budget. Put the PKR 740,000 freed from the Kill campaigns into the biggest proven winner at scale instead, Influencer Fest — Ayesha (ROAS 6.76x, PKR 2,028,600 on PKR 300,000 spend). At the Scale campaigns' average ROAS of 4.24 that money returns about PKR 3,137,600 instead of PKR 637,400.**
3. **Fix TikTok before spending more on it: 164,779 clicks — the most traffic in the account — at a 0.51% conversion rate, the lowest of any paid channel. The clicks are cheap (PKR 8 each) but they do not buy: CPA is PKR 1,631. Send TikTok traffic to a dedicated landing page and tighten the audience before the next rupee goes in.**
4. **Grow Email — the best ROAS in the account (15.98x) on only PKR 83,000 spend. It returned PKR 1,326,100 from 301 orders, but the list is too small to move the business. Grow the list (checkout opt-in, Eid / sale captures) before raising email spend.**
5. **Hold the proven Search / Shopping core and watch the two mid-table campaigns: Google Search is the biggest revenue channel (PKR 5,153,300 at ROAS 3.51); Eid Collection — Shopping and Ramadan Festive Push sit at ROAS 2.43–2.66 — profitable, but below the Scale bar. Re-check them after the next flight before raising their budgets.**
