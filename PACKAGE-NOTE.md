# P23 — Package Note (Fiverr-ready)

**Gig title:** I will track your marketing campaign ROI and tell you which ads to kill, scale or keep

**What the client gets:** a marketing campaign ROI tracker for
their business — every campaign's CTR, CPC, CPA, conversion rate
and ROAS, a Kill / Scale / Keep call on each campaign with the
rule stated, gross-profit-adjusted payback time for every
campaign, the channel rollup, the monthly trend, and the funnel
from impressions to orders, with the reallocation maths beside
the recommendations so they can be checked. Delivered as a 2-page
PDF report plus a live Excel workbook they can re-run on next
month's ad-platform export themselves.

## Tiers (Hashir's band)

| Tier | Price | Delivery | Includes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | $20 | 2 days | The 2-page PDF report on up to 10 campaigns: total spend, total revenue, overall ROAS, the campaign table with Kill / Scale / Keep calls, the channel table, the 5 recommendations |
| Standard | $65 | 3 days | Everything in Basic + up to 50 campaigns, cost per order and payback days per campaign, the monthly trend, the funnel from impressions to orders, the live Excel workbook with formulas |
| Premium | $140 | 5 days | Everything in Standard + up to 200 campaigns, the reallocation plan (what the freed budget returns at the winners' ROAS), a fix-first plan for the weakest channel, a reusable campaign template for their own future ad exports, 1 revision round |

**What's NOT included:** running their ad accounts or talking to
their ad platforms (they run their own campaigns — quoted
separately if they need the account structure written); changing
their prices or product margins (this service shows them which
campaigns pay back at the margin they give); a live dashboard
that updates itself (this service is a report + a workbook they
re-run — a live dashboard is a different gig).

**Delivery time note:** counts from the day the client uploads
their ad-platform export, not from order placement.
